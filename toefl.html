What is TOEFL?

The Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) is the most widely respected English-language test in the world, recognized by more than 11,500 universities and other institutions in over 160 countries.

TOEFL iBT measures your ability to use and understand English at the university level. It evaluates how well you combine your reading, listening, speaking and writing skills to perform academic tasks.

Test Structure

📖 Reading

35 minutes
  • 2 passages
  • 10 questions each
  • Academic texts
  • Score: 0-30

🎧 Listening

36 minutes
  • 3 lectures
  • 2 conversations
  • 28 questions total
  • Score: 0-30

💬 Speaking

16 minutes
  • 4 tasks
  • Independent & integrated
  • Record responses
  • Score: 0-30

✍️ Writing

29 minutes
  • 2 tasks
  • Integrated & academic
  • Type responses
  • Score: 0-30

Expert Tips for TOEFL Success

01

Familiarize with Format

Understand the iBT format and question types. Take free practice tests on the official TOEFL website.

02

Build Academic Vocabulary

Focus on academic English used in university settings. Read scholarly articles and academic texts regularly.

03

Practice Note-Taking

Develop efficient note-taking skills for the listening section. Practice summarizing key points quickly.

04

Master Integrated Tasks

Practice combining information from multiple sources. Learn to synthesize reading and listening materials.

05

Improve Typing Speed

Since TOEFL is computer-based, practice typing your responses quickly and accurately.

06

Use Templates

Develop response templates for speaking and writing tasks to save time and organize thoughts better.
